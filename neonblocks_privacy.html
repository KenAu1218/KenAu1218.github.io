Neon Blocks Privacy Policy

Effective 27 November 2026

In short: Neon Blocks has no accounts and never asks for your name or email. Your settings and local scores stay on your device. Only a few things leave it: your best scores for the global rankings (with a random install ID and an alias the app makes up), the ad data Google uses to show ads, and, if you play a Bluetooth battle, game messages sent straight to the other player's device.

Who we are

Neon Blocks is a falling-block puzzle game for Android and iOS made by Ken Au ("we", "us"). On the App Store it is listed as Neon Blocks: Puzzle Battle, and on your home screen it appears as Neon Blocks. You can contact us at cac2d01@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

  • Your language (English or Traditional Chinese), music settings and local best scores are saved only on your device. We cannot see them.
  • Gameplay itself, including difficulty modes, custom mode, hold, ghost piece, the next queue and touch controls, runs on your device.
  • The synthwave music is generated on your device. You can control it in the game.

Global best-score rankings

To show global rankings, Neon Blocks sends a small amount of data to our leaderboard service, which runs on Supabase:

  • a random installation identifier that the app creates. It does not come from your device's hardware identifiers and is not linked to any login;
  • a public alias the app generates for you. You can't type in your own public name, so your real name is never shown;
  • the game mode and your best score for that mode.

Nothing else is sent to the leaderboard. Your language, music settings and local scores stay on your device.

Bluetooth battles and VS BOT

  • Local two-player battles over Bluetooth are optional. During a battle, the app uses the names of devices already paired with yours, and game messages pass directly between the two devices.
  • Battle messages are not sent to the leaderboard service.
  • VS BOT mode lets you play alone against the computer.

Trophy cards and sharing

After a run you can make a neon trophy card and share it using your device's share sheet. The card goes only where you choose to send it. The app or service you pick has its own privacy policy.

Ads

Neon Blocks shows ads from Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads). To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (on iPhone, only if you allow tracking when asked);
  • your IP address and the approximate location it reveals (country or city level);
  • device information such as model, operating system and language;
  • how you interact with ads, such as views and taps.

Google handles this data under its own privacy policy. You can read how Google uses it at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices:

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Neon Blocks asks for your privacy choices before ads use this data. It does this through Google's User Messaging Platform consent form.
  • On iPhone, you can allow or refuse tracking when asked. You can change this later in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Google → Ads).

What we don't do

  • We don't ask for your name, email or any account.
  • We don't use analytics or tracking tools of our own. The only data shared is what is described above for ads (AdMob and its consent form) and the leaderboard.
  • We don't sell your personal information.

Children

Neon Blocks is not directed at children under 13 (or under the age of digital consent in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, please contact us.

Keeping and deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes the settings and local scores stored on your device. Leaderboard data is kept by Supabase, and advertising data is kept by Google, each under their own policies. You can manage your Google ad settings at myadcenter.google.com.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, the UK, California or Hong Kong), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to its use. Requests about ads are best made to Google. If you have any question about your data, including leaderboard entries, please contact us and we will help.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Important changes will also be mentioned in the app's update notes.
