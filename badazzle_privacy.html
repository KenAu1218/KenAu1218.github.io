Bedazzle Privacy Policy

Effective 15 November 2026

In short: Bedazzle has no accounts and never uploads your photos or artwork. Everything you make stays on your device. The only data that leaves your device is what Google's advertising service uses to show ads (for players without Premium) and what Apple or Google handle when you buy Premium.

Who we are

Bedazzle is a gem art app for Android and iOS made by Ken Au ("we", "us"). You can contact us at cac2d01@gmail.com.

Your photos and artwork

  • When you choose a photo or take one with the camera, Bedazzle turns it into a gem picture on your device. The photo is never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Your works in progress, finished pictures and settings (colour, music, language) are saved only on your device. We cannot see them.
  • The camera is used only while you are taking a photo for a picture, and only after you allow it.
  • When you share or save a finished picture, it goes only where you choose to send it.

Ads

If you don't have Premium, Bedazzle shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (on iPhone, only if you allow tracking when asked),
  • your IP address and approximate location (country or city level),
  • device information such as model, operating system and language,
  • how you interact with ads (for example, views and taps).

Google acts under its own privacy policy. You can read how Google uses this data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices:

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Bedazzle asks for your consent before ads use your data, through Google's consent form. You can change your choice any time in Settings → Privacy choices.
  • On iPhone, you can allow or refuse tracking when asked, and change it later in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Google → Ads).
  • Bedazzle Premium removes ads completely.

Premium purchases

Premium is sold through the Apple App Store or Google Play. They handle the payment and your payment details; we never receive your card or bank information. The app only receives confirmation of whether your subscription is active. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your App Store or Google Play account settings.

Daily photo count

To apply the free daily limit on your own photos, Bedazzle keeps a small count of today's photos on your device. It is not sent anywhere.

What we don't do

  • We don't ask for your name, email or any account.
  • We don't use analytics or tracking tools of our own.
  • We don't sell your personal information.

Children

Bedazzle is not directed at children under 13 (or under the age of digital consent in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app's ads, please contact us.

Keeping and deleting your data

Everything Bedazzle stores is on your device. Deleting the app deletes your works, settings and photo count. Data collected by Google for ads is kept under Google's policies; you can manage it at myadcenter.google.com.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, the UK, California or Hong Kong), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to its use. As we don't hold personal data about you ourselves, most requests are best made to Google for ads, or to Apple or Google for purchases. You are welcome to contact us with any question and we will help.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Important changes will also be mentioned in the app's update notes.
